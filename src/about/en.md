I'm a full-stack software engineer. I work mostly with **Laravel and Vue**, and with **.NET** when the problem calls for a desktop application. I don't stop at the code: I deploy, operate and maintain what I build.

Since 2025 I've worked as a full-stack developer at a nearshore services company, on a team that maintains its internal platform (Laravel, Vue and Active Directory). In parallel I build and run my own projects, several of them in production with real users.

## How I work

- **Understand first, then change.** When something breaks, I look for the cause with evidence (logs, `tcpdump`, the real value of a variable) before touching code. The case studies on this site are written that way on purpose.
- **Tests are part of the job.** My projects have hundreds of automated tests. When a test does not guarantee something, the test itself says so.
- **Security by design:** explicit authorization on every route, least-privilege database users, headers and CSP, and an audit trail for sensitive actions.
- **Document the why.** Every deployment has its guide and its rollback path, and every non-obvious decision is written down next to the code.

## Infrastructure
